# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

**Company:** Cashpile  
**Effective Date:** April 26, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Data Retention and Deletion Policy defines how Cashpile retains, reviews, and deletes personal information and consumer financial data, including data received through Plaid. The purpose of this policy is to ensure that data is retained only as long as necessary for legitimate business, legal, tax, accounting, security, and regulatory purposes, and deleted securely when no longer needed.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Customer account information
- Consumer financial data received through Plaid
- Transaction and account records
- Application logs containing user-related information
- Backups and system snapshots
- Internal records associated with support, security, and compliance

---

## 3. Retention Principles

Cashpile follows these retention principles:

- Retain only the minimum data reasonably necessary to provide services
- Retain data only for defined business, legal, or regulatory purposes
- Delete or de-identify data when it is no longer required
- Restrict access to retained data using least-privilege controls
- Review retention practices periodically

---

## 4. Data Categories and Retention

| Data Category | Examples | Retention Standard |
|---------------|----------|--------------------|
| Account profile data | Name, email, login profile | Retained while account is active and for a reasonable period after closure for legal, security, and operational purposes |
| Plaid-linked account metadata | Institution name, account identifiers, masked details | Retained while the connection is active and as needed to support user history, compliance, and dispute handling |
| Transaction data | Imported transactions, balances, categorization data | Retained while the account is active and thereafter as required for financial reporting, tax, audit, legal, and operational purposes |
| Access tokens and secrets | Plaid access tokens, integration secrets | Retained only as long as needed to maintain active integrations and operations |
| Support and audit records | Support tickets, audit logs, security events | Retained according to operational and compliance needs |
| Backups | Encrypted system backups | Retained for a limited rolling period, generally up to 30 days unless a longer period is required for recovery or legal reasons |

---

## 5. Deletion Triggers

Cashpile initiates deletion, deactivation, or restricted retention when one or more of the following occurs:

- A user requests account deletion
- A linked financial account is disconnected
- Data is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected
- Retention obligations expire
- A legal or compliance review permits deletion

Cashpile may retain limited data after deletion requests where required for:

- Legal compliance
- Tax and accounting obligations
- Fraud prevention and security
- Dispute resolution
- Enforcement of agreements

---

## 6. Secure Deletion Practices

Cashpile uses reasonable administrative and technical measures to securely delete or render inaccessible data that is no longer required.

These practices include:

- Deleting data from active application records
- Revoking access tokens when integrations are disconnected or no longer needed
- Limiting residual copies to backup retention windows
- Restricting access to data pending scheduled backup expiration

Where immediate deletion from backups is not technically feasible, data remains protected until backup expiration and normal overwrite cycles complete.

---

## 7. Consumer Requests

Cashpile supports reasonable requests to:

- Delete account information
- Disconnect linked financial accounts
- Remove stored personal information where permitted by law

Cashpile reviews requests in light of applicable legal, security, tax, and operational obligations before final deletion.

---

## 8. Privacy Law Compliance

This policy is intended to support compliance with applicable privacy and data protection laws, including, where applicable:

- GDPR
- CCPA and CPRA
- Other applicable U.S. state privacy laws

Cashpile applies this policy in conjunction with its Privacy Policy and Information Security Policy.

---

## 9. Review and Governance

This policy is reviewed periodically and at least annually, or sooner if:

- Product data practices change
- New vendors or systems are introduced
- Legal or regulatory requirements change
- Plaid or other partners require updated documentation

---

## 10. Responsibilities

Cashpile personnel responsible for operations, security, and product administration are responsible for:

- Following approved retention periods
- Processing deletion requests appropriately
- Protecting retained data with access controls
- Reviewing retention obligations periodically
