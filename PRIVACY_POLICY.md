# Privacy Policy

**Company:** Cashpile  
**Effective Date:** April 26, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Introduction

Cashpile respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Cashpile collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the Cashpile application, including when you connect financial accounts through Plaid.

By using Cashpile, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Cashpile may collect the following categories of information:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- Name
- Email address
- Account credentials for Cashpile
- Support communications

### 2.2 Information Collected Through Plaid

If you choose to connect a financial account through Plaid, Cashpile may receive:

- Financial account identifiers
- Account names and masked account numbers
- Account balances
- Transaction history
- Institution name
- Other financial data you authorize Plaid to share

Cashpile does **not** collect or store your bank login credentials. Your credentials are entered directly into Plaid Link or your financial institution’s authentication flow.

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

- Device and browser information
- IP address
- Log and usage data
- Session and authentication information

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Cashpile uses personal and financial information to:

- Provide and operate the Cashpile application
- Link and maintain connected financial accounts
- Import, categorize, and display financial data
- Support bookkeeping, reporting, and related product features
- Maintain security, detect fraud, and prevent misuse
- Communicate with users about accounts, support, and service updates
- Comply with legal obligations

Cashpile uses consumer financial data only for purposes authorized by the user and consistent with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

Cashpile does not sell consumer financial data.

Cashpile may share information only in the following circumstances:

- With **service providers** that help operate the application, such as hosting, database, analytics, authentication, and infrastructure vendors
- With **Plaid**, in connection with the operation of linked financial account features
- When required by **law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process**
- To protect the rights, security, and integrity of Cashpile, its users, or others
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable confidentiality and privacy obligations

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Cashpile uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal and financial information, including:

- Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or better
- Encryption at rest through managed infrastructure providers
- Role-based and least-privilege access controls
- Authenticated access to protected application areas
- Secure handling of environment secrets and tokens

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of storage is completely secure, but Cashpile uses commercially reasonable safeguards to protect data.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Cashpile retains personal and financial information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Maintain linked account functionality
- Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Detailed retention and deletion practices are described in Cashpile’s `DATA_RETENTION_AND_DELETION_POLICY.md`.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Request information about how data is collected, used, and shared

You may also disconnect linked financial accounts and request deletion of your account, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.
